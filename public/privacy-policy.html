Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Introduction

CERARIA Private Limited ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website ceraria.in (the "Site"). By using the Site, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information when you voluntarily provide it to us through our contact and inquiry forms:

  • Name — to address you in communications
  • Email address — to respond to your inquiries
  • Phone number — to contact you regarding product inquiries
  • Message content — details about your product interest or inquiry
  • Product name and quantity — to process bulk order inquiries

We do not collect payment information, passwords, government-issued IDs, or any sensitive personal data through the Site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To process and fulfill product consultation requests
  • To communicate with you about our products and services
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law

4. Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored on secure cloud servers hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS). We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, access controls, and secure communication protocols (HTTPS) to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With trusted service providers who assist us in operating the Site (e.g., hosting providers), bound by confidentiality obligations
  • When required by law, court order, or governmental regulation
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of CERARIA, our users, or others

6. Cookies

Our Site uses essential cookies only for session management (e.g., admin authentication). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics that monitor your browsing behavior across websites.

7. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and applicable Indian laws, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@ceraria.in.

8. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date. Continued use of the Site after any modifications constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
