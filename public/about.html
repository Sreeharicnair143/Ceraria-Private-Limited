Our Story

Architecture in India has always been an act of storytelling — the courtyards of heritage havelis, the verandahs of colonial bungalows, the clean lines of contemporary urban homes. Every era has asked the same question of its surfaces:
does this space feel as good as it looks?

Ceraria was created to answer that question for modern India. We are not a tile manufacturer — we are curators of surfaces, sourcing the finest architectural tiles from India's leading production houses and respected global ceramic ateliers, and bringing them to India's architects, builders, and homeowners through partnerships with the country's most trusted dealers. The name Ceraria draws from ceramica — the ancient art of fired earth — reimagined for a generation of Indian homes and spaces that expect more from every surface they build on.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver curated, world-class ceramic and tile collections through trusted dealer partnerships — combining lasting quality, refined design, and dependable service for every architect, builder, and homeowner we serve.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in ceramic luxury — the first choice for anyone who believes a space should be extraordinary.

What We Believe

Luxury is chosen.

Every Ceraria collection is selected, not produced — which means our only obligation is to design, quality, and durability, not production convenience. We choose what belongs in Indian homes and buildings, not what is easiest to make.

Engineered for India's climate.

From coastal humidity to northern winters, from monsoon intensity to peak summer heat, Indian buildings demand materials that perform, not just impress. Every tile is chosen for its ability to withstand India's range of climates without compromising on design.

Global craftsmanship.

From large-format porcelain slabs to natural stone-inspired finishes and heritage-pattern-influenced designs, our collections speak two languages at once: the international vocabulary of contemporary architecture, and the aesthetic instincts that define Indian spaces.

Who We Serve

Ceraria exists for the architect designing a space that must last generations. For the builder delivering a project that needs to stand out. For the homeowner who wants their home to feel considered, not just constructed. For the dealer who wants a name on their showroom floor that signals taste, not just stock. We partner only with India's leading tile dealers — because a brand's reputation is built one showroom, one project, one conversation at a time.
